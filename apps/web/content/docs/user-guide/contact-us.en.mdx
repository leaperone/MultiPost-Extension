## How to Ask Questions
If you run into issues in foreground dispatch (publishing flow mode)—for example, you cannot publish, your content does not appear, the flow gets stuck, or fields fail to fill—please feel free to report it. To help us reproduce and improve the issue faster, include your environment, extension version, target platform, reproduction steps, and any screenshots or error messages when available. You can also refer to [How To Ask Questions The Smart Way](https://github.com/ryanhanwu/How-To-Ask-Questions-The-Smart-Way/blob/main/README-zh_CN.md) to make the report easier to follow.

## What Questions Can You Contact Us About
- Request to add platforms you commonly use
