# MultiPost vs Hootsuite: Free vs $739/month

**TL;DR:** Hootsuite is enterprise software ($99-739+/month) with social listening, team workflows, and compliance features. MultiPost is a free browser extension for publishing to 50+ platforms. Most individuals and small teams don't need (or use) Hootsuite's enterprise features. [Skip to verdict](#the-verdict).

## The Reality Check

Hootsuite offers:
- Social listening and sentiment analysis
- Team approval workflows
- Client management for agencies
- Compliance and audit trails
- Advanced analytics

**Question:** How many people paying $99-739/month actually use these features?

My guess: not many. Most just schedule posts. That's expensive post scheduling.

## When You Actually Need Hootsuite

- Large organization with compliance requirements
- Audit trails for legal/regulatory reasons
- Managing client accounts as an agency
- Social listening and brand monitoring
- Multiple teams needing approval workflows

## When You Don't Need Hootsuite

- Solo creator or small team
- Just publishing content (not monitoring)
- No regulatory compliance requirements
- Budget matters
- Need Chinese platforms (Hootsuite doesn't support them anyway)

## The Chinese Platform Gap

Hootsuite supports zero Chinese platforms. No Weibo, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Bilibili, or WeChat.

If you need any of those, Hootsuite can't help. MultiPost covers all of them. Free.

## The Verdict

**Choose Hootsuite if:**
- You're an enterprise with compliance needs
- You need social listening and sentiment analysis
- You manage client accounts
- You need team approval workflows
- Budget isn't a constraint

**Choose MultiPost if:**
- You're an individual or small team
- You just want to publish content
- You need Chinese platforms
- You want free (actually free, not "free tier")
- You need API access without enterprise pricing
