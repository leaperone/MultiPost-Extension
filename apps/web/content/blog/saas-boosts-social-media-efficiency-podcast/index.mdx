# From 15 Hours to 3: How We Fixed Our Content Distribution Problem

**TL;DR:** Our team was spending 15 hours/week copying content between platforms. We switched to MultiPost and got it down to under 3 hours. Here's what changed.

---

## The Problem

Our small team creates 3-4 pieces of content weekly. Each piece goes to:

- Zhihu
- Bilibili
- Weibo
- Twitter
- LinkedIn
- Sometimes Xiaohongshu
- Sometimes YouTube

That's 6-8 platforms per piece. Each with different formatting rules, character limits, image ratios, and video restrictions.

**We tracked it once: 15 hours a week on distribution alone.**

Not creating. Not engaging. Just copying content from one place to another.

That's almost half a full-time job, spent on copy-paste.

## What Didn't Work

**Manual efficiency improvements:** Templates, batch processing. Still took forever.

**Scheduling tools:** Helped with timing, but we still had to format everything separately. Most didn't support Chinese platforms anyway.

**Hiring someone:** Worked, but paying a person for mechanical copy-paste felt wasteful.

## What Actually Worked

[MultiPost](https://multipost.app?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=efficiency-story&utm_content=inline).

**Our new workflow:**

1. Write in the Markdown editor
2. Generate a cover with the built-in AI
3. Select 6-8 platforms
4. Click publish
5. Done in 5 minutes

From 15 hours to under 3 hours weekly. That's 12 hours back.

## The Results

**Before:**
- 15 hours/week on distribution
- 3 platforms active (couldn't keep up with more)
- Team burned out after 6 months

**After:**
- Under 3 hours/week
- 6-8 platforms active
- Sustainable indefinitely

Reach went up about 40%. Engagement up about 25%. But the biggest change: we don't hate distribution anymore. It's just... handled.

## What We Do With Those 12 Hours

Better content.

When you're not exhausted from distribution busywork, you have energy for creative work. We write better. We respond to comments more. We actually engage instead of just broadcasting.

We also started looking at analytics. Turns out Zhihu drives way more engagement for us than Weibo. We wouldn't have noticed that if we were still drowning in distribution work.
