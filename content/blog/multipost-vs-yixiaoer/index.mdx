## What Each Does Better

### Yixiaoer wins at:

- **Platform count** — 60+ vs 50+
- **Team collaboration** — Approval workflows, multi-account management
- **Enterprise features** — Content quality checking, team management
- **Chinese platform depth** — Mature integrations

### MultiPost wins at:

- **Price** — Free vs freemium
- **AI tools** — Built-in image and poster generation
- **Developer experience** — APIs for automation
- **Markdown support** — Native editor
- **International platforms** — Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube
- **No registration** — Uses browser sessions

## The Verdict

**Choose Yixiaoer if:**
- You're a team or agency needing approval workflows
- Maximum platform count matters (60+)
- You prefer traditional rich text editing
- Enterprise support is important

**Choose MultiPost if:**
- You're a solo creator or developer
- Free matters (it's completely free)
- You want AI image generation built in
- You write in Markdown
- You want APIs for automation
- You publish internationally, not just to Chinese platforms

---

**Neither is universally "better."** They're built for different users. Teams and enterprises → Yixiaoer. Individuals and developers → MultiPost.
